What the app is for

  • Attendance

    QR scan or a typed Company ID. The exact time is kept.

  • Records

    Personal records and weekly payroll figures are stored. Nothing here transfers money.

  • Requests

    Leave and corrections move by role. Cash advances are not part of this app.

  • Messages

    Direct messages and site announcements, written once.

  • Android

    One signed app for 64-bit and older 32-bit ARM phones. It always asks permission, then installs when you allow it.

  • By role

    Employee, Site Head, HR, CEO and developer each open only what that role is allowed to see.

Who opens what

  • Employee

    Attendance, own records, requests and messages.

  • Site Head

    Team attendance, site updates and manpower notes.

  • HR

    Employee records, onboarding, approvals and payroll preparation.

  • CEO

    Company overview, reports, approvals and monitoring.

The developer maintains the system. That access does not open payroll records. AI Workspace, where a role is allowed to use it, opens Google AI Studio or Gemini on that person’s own Google account.

Talk through the job before work starts, build what the company is engaged to construct, and hand the finished work over with the records that belong to it. Named projects appear only after the company confirms them.
